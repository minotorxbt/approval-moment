Approval Moment

Before an AI assistant pays on your behalf, a separate verifier compares its payment plan with what you asked for.

1. Pick a scenario

2. Conversation with the assistant

3. Payment plan prepared by the assistant

4. Page the assistant read

5. Verifier result

Prototype: shopping, delivery and dispute steps are simulated; no real money is spent. If the server is running and a NEAR AI key is set, the verifier also uses NEAR AI Cloud.
